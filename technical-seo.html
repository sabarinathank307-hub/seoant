Technical SEO FAQs

What does a technical SEO review cover?

It can cover crawl and indexation signals, internal linking, site structure, redirects, XML sitemaps, mobile usability, page performance, and structured data. Scope depends on the site and the questions you need answered.

Will technical SEO guarantee higher rankings?

No. Technical improvements can help search engines access and interpret a site, but rankings also depend on relevance, competition, content quality, and many other factors. No particular ranking can be guaranteed.

Do I need a developer to implement technical SEO?

Some changes are straightforward, while others require a developer or platform specialist. We explain the requirements and can help your team understand which work needs technical support.
