E-commerce SEO FAQs

What does e-commerce SEO include?

It may include product and category keyword mapping, page content improvements, internal linking, indexation and duplicate URL reviews, structured data recommendations, and site performance checks.

Should every product page be indexed?

Not always. Indexation choices depend on whether a page is useful, unique, available, and valuable to searchers. Product variants, filters, and temporary stock changes should be handled according to the store's catalog and customer needs.

Can SEO help a small online store?

Yes. A smaller store can focus on its most valuable categories and products, improve their information, and build a sound foundation before expanding into broader catalog work.
