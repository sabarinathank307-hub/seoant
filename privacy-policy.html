Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 21 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how SEOAnt ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects personal information when you visit our website or use our services. By accessing or using this website, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

When you submit an enquiry through our contact form or communicate with us via email, we may collect the following personal information:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your phone number
  • Any additional information you include in your message

We collect this information only when you voluntarily submit it through the contact form or direct communication channels listed on this website.

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

When you visit our website, your web browser may automatically send certain standard information to our web server, such as your IP address, browser type, referring page, and the pages you visit. This information is typical of standard web server logs and is not used by us to personally identify you.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the personal information you provide for the following purposes:

  • To respond to your enquiries and service requests
  • To provide information about our SEO services
  • To prepare and deliver free SEO audit reports you have requested
  • To communicate with you about potential or ongoing projects
  • To improve our website and service offerings

We do not use your personal information for automated decision-making or profiling.

3. Google Search Console

As part of our SEO services, we may request access to your Google Search Console account or ask you to grant us access. Google Search Console is a tool provided by Google that allows website owners and authorised users to monitor and manage a website's presence in Google Search results.

When we use Google Search Console on your behalf, we access data such as:

  • Search queries that lead visitors to your website
  • Click-through rates, impressions, and average position data
  • Indexing status and crawl information
  • Manual actions or security issues reported by Google

Google Search Console data is governed by Google's Privacy Policy. We do not control how Google collects or processes data within Search Console. We use this data solely to analyse and improve your website's SEO performance as part of the services you have engaged us to provide.

Note: Google Search Console is not the same as Google Analytics. Google Search Console provides data about your website's visibility in Google Search. Google Analytics is a separate service that tracks and reports website visitor behaviour. This website does not currently use Google Analytics.

4. Cookies and Third-Party Services

Our website may use essential cookies that are necessary for the website to function correctly. These cookies do not collect personal information for marketing or tracking purposes.

Our website loads certain resources from third-party content delivery networks (CDNs) for displaying tool logos and icons. These third-party services may collect standard web request information (such as your IP address) when your browser requests these resources. We do not control the data collection practices of these third-party services.

If we introduce any new cookies or third-party tracking services in the future, we will update this policy accordingly.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share your information only in the following circumstances:

  • Service providers: We may use third-party services (such as email hosting providers) to help us operate our business. These providers are given access to your information only as necessary to perform their functions and are obligated to maintain confidentiality.
  • Legal requirements: We may disclose your information if required to do so by law, regulation, or legal process, or if we believe disclosure is necessary to protect our rights, your safety, or the safety of others.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your personal information from unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Data Retention

We retain your personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected, including responding to your enquiry and providing our services. Once the information is no longer needed, we will take reasonable steps to delete or anonymise it.

8. Your Rights

Under applicable Indian law, including the Information Technology Act, 2000, and the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011, you have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Right to access: You may request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Right to correction: You may request that we correct any inaccurate or incomplete personal information.
  • Right to withdrawal of consent: You may withdraw your consent for the collection and use of your personal information at any time by contacting us.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details provided below. We will respond to your request within a reasonable timeframe.

9. Children's Privacy

Our website and services are not directed at individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will take steps to delete such information.
