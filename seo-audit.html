SEO audit FAQs

What does an SEO audit review?

A review can examine crawlability, indexation, site structure, page-level elements, content, internal links, and available organic performance data. Audit depth is agreed based on your site and needs.

How long does an SEO audit take?

Timing depends on website size, complexity, data access, and agreed scope. SEOAnt will clarify the expected process and deliverables before work begins.

Does an audit include implementing the recommendations?

An audit primarily identifies and prioritizes opportunities. Implementation support can be discussed separately, depending on the work required and your team's preferences.
